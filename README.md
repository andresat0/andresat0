# Andres Aguirre Torres

I'm an M.S. Financial Engineering student at **NYU Tandon**. I earned B.S. degrees in **Mathematics, Finance, and Economics** at Penn State, where I was a Schreyer Honors Scholar.

I'm interested in quantitative research, especially empirical finance, time-series methods, and numerical approaches to derivatives pricing.

### Research and projects

**[FOMC Variance Research — Honors Thesis](https://github.com/andresat0/fomc-variance-research)** — I study how monetary-policy surprises and FOMC statement tone relate to S&P 500 implied and subsequent realized variance, using event studies, principal component analysis, and controlled regressions. My [submitted thesis is available through Penn State University Libraries](https://honors.libraries.psu.edu/catalog/10189ava6461); the repository contains my original analysis and current research status.

**[Monte Carlo Option Pricing](https://github.com/andresat0/monte-carlo-option-pricing)** — Building on my mathematics honors projects, I compare European-call estimates under GBM and Merton jump diffusion with analytic benchmarks. I examine how antithetic variance reduction changes sampling error at equal simulation budgets through reproducible experiments.

**[U.S. Trade-Balance Forecasting](https://github.com/andresat0/trade-balance-forecasting)** — I revisit my Stata coursework with a Python comparison across 200 historical forecast origins. I compare autoregressive models with a last-observation baseline, check prediction-interval coverage, and report where added complexity does and does not help.

**[Iris Classification](https://github.com/andresat0/ML-Projects)** — I practice the basics of classification with an introductory decision-tree exercise.
