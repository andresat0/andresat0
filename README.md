# Andres Aguirre Torres

I'm an M.S. Financial Engineering student at **NYU Tandon**. I earned B.S. degrees in **Mathematics, Finance, and Economics** at Penn State, where I was a Schreyer Honors Scholar.

I'm interested in quantitative research, especially empirical finance, time-series methods, and numerical approaches to derivatives pricing.

### Featured project

**[Monte Carlo Option Pricing](https://github.com/andresat0/monte-carlo-option-pricing)** — I compare European-call estimates under GBM and Merton jump diffusion using analytic benchmarks, corrected antithetic uncertainty, and reproducible experiments. I examine how variance reduction changes sampling error at equal simulation budgets.

### Research and academic work

- **FOMC announcements and equity variance:** In my honors thesis, I studied S&P 500 implied and subsequent realized variance around monetary-policy announcements using event studies, principal component analysis, and controlled regressions.
- **Monte Carlo option pricing:** My mathematics honors projects explored European-call simulation under geometric Brownian motion and Merton jump diffusion, with analytic benchmarks and sampling-error analysis.
- **U.S. trade-balance forecasting:** I used autoregressive models in Stata to generate forecasts and evaluate a subsequent observation.

The option-pricing study is available above. I'm preparing the empirical research and forecasting work as documented repositories.
